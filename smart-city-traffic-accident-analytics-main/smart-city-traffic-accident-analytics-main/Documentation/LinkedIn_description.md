Excited to share my semester project: **Smart City Traffic & Accident Analytics**!

Key highlights:
- Analyzed 20,000 accident records across 8 Indian metropolitan cities
- Discovered that weather conditions drive 46% of accident risk variance (ANOVA η² = 0.4612)
- Rush-hour accidents have 57.7% higher risk than off-peak periods
- Built a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard with 25+ DAX measures
- Trained Decision Tree and Random Forest classifiers achieving 88.88% accuracy with zero catastrophic errors
- Technologies: Python, Pandas, Power BI, DAX, Scikit-learn
