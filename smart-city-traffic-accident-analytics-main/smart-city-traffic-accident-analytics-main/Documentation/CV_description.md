# Smart City Traffic & Accident Analytics — CV & Resume Description

## One-Line Description

Built an end-to-end Smart City Traffic & Accident Analytics platform analyzing 20,000 accident records across 8 Indian cities using Python, Power BI, and machine learning to identify risk patterns and predict collision severity.

## Resume Bullet Points

- Designed and developed an interactive 5-page Power BI dashboard with 25+ DAX measures for real-time traffic accident monitoring across 8 metropolitan cities (20,000 records, 34 features)
- Performed comprehensive EDA and statistical analysis uncovering that weather conditions explain 46% of risk score variance (ANOVA F=8,559, p<0.001) and visibility has r=-0.73 inverse correlation with risk
- Trained Decision Tree and Random Forest classifiers achieving 88.88% accuracy in multi-class collision risk prediction with zero catastrophic false negatives across 4,000 test observations
